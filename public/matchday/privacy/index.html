Privacy policy

How the Matchday app handles information while creating and maintaining football match threads on Reddit.

Effective 18 September 2026

1. About this app

MCFCBot Matchday is an independent app built on Reddit’s Developer Platform. It creates and maintains football match threads for communities where it is installed.

The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Reddit, Manchester City Football Club or any football data provider.

2. Information we process

The app processes limited community, post and operational information supplied by Reddit. This includes community settings, identifiers for content created by the app and the context needed to authorise moderator controls.

3. How information is used and stored

Information is used only to provide the match-thread service, prevent duplicate posts, maintain community features and diagnose problems.

App settings and service state are stored within Reddit’s Developer Platform and retained only for as long as they are needed to operate the app reliably.

4. External services

Reddit hosts the app and supplies the information it is authorised to use. The app also uses football data services to provide match information.

Cloudflare Pages hosts this policy website and may process routine web-request information under Cloudflare’s own privacy policy.

5. Advertising and tracking

The app does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. This website has no analytics, marketing cookies, forms or client-side tracking.

6. Your choices and rights

You can manage your Reddit content through Reddit’s normal controls and contact the moderators of the relevant community about an app-created thread. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights relating to your personal information.

Requests about information controlled by Reddit should be directed to Reddit. Questions about this app can be sent by modmail to r/mcfcbot.

7. Changes

We may update this policy if the app or its legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
