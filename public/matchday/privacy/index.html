Privacy policy

How the Matchday app handles information while creating and maintaining football match threads on Reddit.

Effective 18 September 2026

1. Who this policy covers

MCFCBot Matchday is an independent community app built on Reddit’s Developer Platform. This policy applies when the app is installed in a supported Reddit community or when a moderator uses its controls.

The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Reddit, Manchester City Football Club, ESPN or any football competition or data provider.

2. Information the app processes

The app processes only the information needed to run its match-thread service:

  • The name and configuration of the Reddit community where it is installed.
  • Fixture identifiers, teams, competitions, kick-off times, scores, events, line-ups, statistics and related football information obtained from configured sports-data services.
  • Reddit identifiers and links for posts and comments created or managed by the app, together with their thread type and lifecycle state.
  • Moderator account and community context supplied by Reddit when a moderator opens or uses an app control, solely to authorise that action.
  • Operational information such as scheduled-job state, provider status, request counts, failures and update timestamps.

The app does not require users to create a separate account, provide an email address or supply payment information. It does not build profiles of supporters or retain the text of ordinary user comments.

3. How information is used

Information is used to discover fixtures; create, update and reconcile pre-match, match and post-match threads; keep graphical match information current; avoid duplicate posts; update approved sidebar widgets; notify moderators of operational issues; and diagnose faults.

4. Storage and retention

Application state is stored in Redis provided as part of Reddit’s Developer Platform. This includes fixture snapshots, app-created post identifiers, scheduler state, settings and operational status.

Fixture snapshots and post identifiers may be retained so historical threads continue to show the match they reference and so retries, restarts or provider changes do not create duplicates. Short-lived locks and processing markers expire automatically. Operational status records are either time-limited or replaced by newer status information.

The app does not operate an external user database and does not copy ordinary Reddit user content to a developer-controlled server.

5. External services

  • Reddit and Devvit: Reddit hosts the app, supplies authorised Reddit data and stores app state. Reddit’s own policies apply to its processing.
  • Sports-data providers: The app makes server-side requests to configured providers for football information. Those requests use fixture, team, competition and date information; Reddit usernames and user content are not sent to those providers.
  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare Pages hosts this policy site and may process routine web-request information such as IP addresses and security logs under its own privacy policy.

6. Advertising, tracking and sale of data

The app contains no advertising, marketing cookies or cross-site tracking. The app operator does not sell personal information. This legal site uses no analytics, forms or client-side scripts.

7. Your choices and rights

Reddit users can control their Reddit content through Reddit’s normal tools and can contact the moderators of the relevant community about an app-created thread. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights concerning personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection.

Requests about information controlled by Reddit should be directed to Reddit. Questions specifically about this app can be sent by modmail to r/mcfcbot.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children outside the information Reddit makes available under its own services and policies.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s functionality, providers or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
